Time to get you some gains!

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice or basmati rice
  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper (to taste)
  • Water or chicken broth (for cooking the rice and chicken)
  • Optional finish: A squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a drizzle of soy sauce / tamari

Instructions:

  1. Cook The Rice
    1. Rinse your rice in a fine-mesh sieve

    2. In a saucepan, combine 1 cup of rice with the appropriate amount of water or chicken stock (usually a 1:2 ratio) and a pinch of salt.

      Bring to a boil, cover with a lid, lower the heat to a gentle simmer, and cook for about 18–20 minutes without stirring. Fluff with a fork.

  2. Prep the Chicken:
    1. Cut the chicken breasts in half lengthwise or pound them to an even thickness so they cook quickly and evenly.
    2. Pat dry with a paper towel and season both sides generously with salt and black pepper.

  3. Sear The Chicken

    1. Heat the olive oil and butter in a skillet over medium heat until the butter melts and starts to lightly foam.
    2. Add the chicken to the pan. Cook for about 3 to 4 minutes on the first side until it releases easily, then flip and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until cooked through (no longer pink inside).

  4. Rest and Slice:

    1. Remove the chicken from the skillet and let it rest for 3 minutes so the juices stay inside. Slice it into bite-sized pieces or strips.

  5. Assemble:

    1. Scoop a generous portion of the warm, fluffy rice into a bowl or plate.
    2. Top with the sliced plain chicken and spoon any pan juices or a squeeze of fresh lemon juice over the top.
