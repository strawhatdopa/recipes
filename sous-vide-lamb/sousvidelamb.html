Time to make a Chef out of you!

Simply follow the instructions below!

Ingredients:

  • Boneless, skinless (or skin-on) chicken breasts
  • Fresh thyme sprigs and smashed garlic cloves
  • Unsalted butter or a light drizzle of high-quality truffle oil (for the vacuum bag), plus a high-smoke-point oil (like canola or avocado oil) for searing
  • Diamond Crystal kosher salt (or fine sea salt) and freshly cracked black pepper
  • Extra butter for basting (beurre noisette) and flaky fleur de sel to finish

Instructions

  1. Pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts to a uniform thickness.
  2. Season generously with high-quality salt (such as Diamond Crystal), fresh cracked pepper, a sprig of fresh thyme, a smashed garlic clove, and a touch of butter or truffle oil inside the bag.
  3. Seal the bag tightly, removing all air to ensure even thermal contact.
  4. Cook the chicken sous vide at 145°F to 155°F (63°C-68°C) for 25 to 35 minutes.
  5. Remove from the bag and pat completely dry with paper towels. Sear skin-side down (if skin-on) or the exterior in a hot skillet with high-smoke-point oil (like canola or avocado oil) until golden and crisp.
  6. Baste with foamy brown butter (beurre noisette) and finish with fleur de sel before slicing.
